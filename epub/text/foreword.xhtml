A wild month and no mistake. We bring you the comforts of literature, poetry, and silly animal videos in a time of global strife and uncertainty, as well as reflections on teaching young journalists to make their way in this new world; protecting the professional integrity of scientists, who are under siege in the United States; the corruption of Jeffrey Epstein and his many eminent friends; a conversation on Osip Mandelstam, art and authoritarianism, and an appreciation of crime novelist Ross Macdonald.

Finally, Zach Rabiroff profiles “Roddy Pinili,” the pseudonym of an immigrant man who was brought at the age of five to the U.S. from the Philippines, and the possibility that he may suddenly become a stateless person in an administration suddenly, incomprehensibly and violently hostile to immigrants.

This is a picture of life in 2025 by writers and artists working to preserve press freedom and freedom of information. Thank you for reading and supporting Flaming Hydra.
