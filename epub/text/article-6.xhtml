Book Review: The Epstein Birthday Book

by Maria Bustillos

BOOK REVIEW: THE FIRST FIFTY YEARS, The Birthday Book of Jeffrey Epstein, 2003; Ghislaine Maxwell, ed.

This soul-poisoning book was compiled and annotated by editor and future convicted sex trafficker Ghislaine Maxwell. Her handwritten captions narrate the book throughout. It consists of notes, letters, and drawings provided by friends and associates⁠—Donald Trump among them⁠—as a 50th birthday gift to Maxwell’s mentor and accomplice, the late Jeffrey Epstein, who would die, reportedly by suicide, in the Metropolitan Correctional Center in New York City in August 2019.

Scans of the 238-page book were provided by the Epstein estate to the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform in response to a subpoena, and yesterday these scans were released to the public.

Maxwell was convicted in 2022 for her role in “a scheme to sexually exploit and abuse multiple minor girls with Jeffrey Epstein over the course of a decade.” She is serving a 20-year prison sentence. Last month the Trump administration moved her from a Florida prison to “a new minimum-security prison in Texas.” There has been a lot of talk about a Trump pardon for Maxwell.

Legacy media has failed to reckon with the contents and significance of the Birthday Book and its publication. First, let’s have a brief look at what CNN, the New York Times, and the Washington Post had to say about it.

Here’s the heading of the email I received, as a subscriber, from the Washington Post on the publication of The First Fifty Years.

The Post reported few details on the contents of the Birthday Book, focusing instead on an alleged controversy regarding the authenticity of Trump’s signature therein⁠—a controversy that was pretty much put to rest by attorney George Conway, who posted a personal letter he received from Trump in 2006 bearing an all-but-identical signature.

Here’s what CNN had to say.

They may not have seen any sign of wrongdoing, but everyone else does.

For once the New York Times ran perhaps the least offensively wrongheaded corporate media take on a Trump story, adding relevant details of the outlandish prurience and offensiveness of the book’s contents. “Many of the contributions are disturbing,” they observed. “In one letter, a person who signed only ‘Nick,’ recounted an evening in London that left Mr. Epstein ‘howling with laughter.’ That night, the contributor said, an ‘old man smiling sweetly’ pulled down a woman’s panties and put his hand on her privates, only to find another man’s hand already there. Another letter alludes to when Mr. Epstein, in the mid-1970s, first ‘discovered the Maxwell teen-age daughter.’ ”

It’s a bit difficult to picture Maxwell in 2003, then the “girlfriend” of Epstein, inviting his friends, relatives, exes, and business associates to contribute these reminiscences, photos, etc. for the Birthday Book. Like I guess she is sitting there with a bottle of Elmer’s Glue or whatever, compiling this scrapbook. The result is a document so depraved and so vile as to cause any regular person to want to defect from the human race as a result of reading it, though major media outlets and the Democratic leadership in the U.S. are refusing to come out and say the obvious.

The Trump administration claims that Donald Trump’s contribution to the book⁠—a line drawing depicting a naked, weirdly armless, prepubescent girl’s body framing a freakish imaginary dialogue between Trump and Epstein⁠—is faked. Here’s what CNN and the Post and the Times aren’t saying: the preponderance of the evidence, published over many years, strongly suggests that the drawing is genuine. It is exactly in keeping with decades of Trump’s gross, inappropriate behavior toward women.

Epstein’s mother Paula provides one of the first entries in the Birthday Book, expressing pride in her son’s accomplishments. “I knew you were an excellent student because your report cards were ‘A plus’⁠ ⁠… at age 27 Cosmopolitan magazine featured you as ‘Bachelor of the Month.’ Today you still hold that title⁠ ⁠… Your experiences in Europe helped you get the job at Dalton [the private school where Epstein taught math from and from which he was fired]. Today⁠—a limousine awaits you as a dignitary.”

Following this there is a collage of photos, autograph books, yearbook entries and the like, followed by an account entitled “Girls on My Boat,” including the following reminiscience: “We picked up girls on Beach⁠—went out on Boat. I tell with knife in my hand to take suits off. But Warren tells Don’t worry his name is J.M. hes just Joking he live at so & so, I tell Mark to throw him into water, he Did⁠ ⁠…”

Next, “Castaways Vol. 1”, with the anecdote, “I was Porking some girl in Bed & Jeff Brings in the maid to make Bed She left screming & never came Back.”

Later there is a colorful and depraved drawing of a blissed-out Epstein reclining on a chaise longue being massaged by four topless blonde ladies. The one attending to his genitals has a heart tattoo on her right buttock with “JE” in the middle.

Next comes a letter dated January 15, 2003, Dallas, Texas, written by “Johnny Boy Kafka” with full address. “Dearest Jeff,” it begins.

“Fifty years old. You really have had a pretty amazing life. Amazing because of where and when it started. Paula and Seymour fucking. Who was there to tell him where to stick it? Who told him to spit on her cunt to make it wet?”

This is his friend’s mom he is talking about. The mind reels at the idea that the men in Epstein’s orbit could have known him as just a rich eccentric with varied cultural interests. That has typically been the defense of people like Alan Dershowitz and Bill Clinton, both of whom contributed to the book.

For those who have heard the Access Hollywood tape and read the details of the many lawsuits filed against Trump by the many women he is alleged to have abused, for anyone who has read the countless gross remarks made by Donald Trump, and the accounts of many, many women who have accused him of sexual misconduct, the Birthday Book will represent the pinnacle of every coarse and distressing thing you already knew. And yet you will read none of that screamingly obvious conclusion in the pages of the nation’s few remaining newspapers.

Donald Trump is unfit to be a head of state for reasons that continue to multiply and metastasize day by day. These gross, vulgar, ignorant, tasteless, abusive, disgusting predators have got to be removed from public life. There is no need to read this book if you can help it, but there is every need to get this man and anyone who has ever been associated with him out of office.
